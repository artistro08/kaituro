# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Messages doesn't collect anything about you. There's no account with us, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your messages go between your PC, Google and your phone, the same way they do in Google Messages for Web.

## What Stays On Your PC

Messages keeps a few files in its own app folder so it can work:

- **Your pairing session**, so you don't have to pair every time you open it. It's encrypted with your Windows account, so only you can read it.
- **Your recent chats**, so they open instantly after a restart. Names, numbers and message text are encrypted with your Windows account too.
- **Your unsent drafts**, so what you typed is still there after you close Messages. They're encrypted with your Windows account like your chats.
- **Contact photos** used in notifications.
- **Your settings**, like your theme and microphone.
- **Log files** to help fix bugs. They never include your messages, contacts or links.

Signing out deletes your session, your saved chats and drafts, and the contact photos. Uninstalling Messages deletes everything.

## Who Messages Talks To

- **Google.** Messages connects to Google Messages to pair with your phone and to send and receive your messages, photos and voice messages. Google's own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) covers that.
- **Websites in links.** When a message has a link, Messages loads that page to show a preview card, just like your phone does. It only loads secure (HTTPS) links, never sends cookies or sign-ins, and keeps previews in memory only. You can turn previews off for links you receive in Settings.

That's it. Nothing is ever sent to us or anyone else.

## Your Microphone

Messages only uses your microphone while you're recording a voice message.
